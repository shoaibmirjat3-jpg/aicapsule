Privacy Policy

AI Capsule Publisher · last updated 25 November 2026

What this app is

AI Capsule Publisher is an internal tool used only by THE AI CAPSULE channel. It runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is not available to other users.

What data it uses

  • YouTube (Google): it uploads videos we made to our own channel, and reads our own channel and video statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): it publishes our own Reels to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page, and reads their statistics.

It does not collect, store or share data about viewers or any other person. It does not read comments or messages.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the channel owner's computer. They are never sold or shared with anyone.

Google API Services

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube use also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions or from Meta's Business Integrations settings.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on the THE AI CAPSULE YouTube channel.
